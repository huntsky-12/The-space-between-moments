THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter VIII

The Ways I Loved Her

It was never just one thing...
It was everything that made her, her...
Begin reading ↓
I — He loved even the things she never noticed.

The Little Things

I loved her in her smile....

In her most weird faces,
in her most nonsense talks,
in those little expressions she made without even knowing.....

In the beautiful way she carried herself,
in her pretty little lips,
in the way they curved when she smiled,
and the little pout they made when she was upset....

I loved her laughter....

The loud one...
The uncontrollable one.
The one she tried to hide and couldn't...

Her random stories.
Her pointless arguments.
Her little complaints.
The things she said that made absolutely no sense....

I could listen to all of it...

II — Sometimes, they said everything.

Her Eyes

I loved her eyes...

Especially when they looked at me.

And I loved those moments when we simply looked at each other.

No words....

Just that quiet kind of eye contact where everything somehow felt understood.

I hated those eyes when they had tears in them...

Hated seeing her cry...

Hated every tear that fell from them...

But I loved the way she came to me when she was hurting...

The way she could be quiet beside me.

The way she trusted me enough to let me see her like that.

III — He loved both sides of her.

Her Silence and Her Noise

I loved her silence...

And I loved the noise in her...

Her endless talking...
Her sudden excitement.
Her random thoughts.
Her little listen, listen, listen moments...

I loved her sleepy voice...

Her tired eyes...

Her messy hair...

Especially when a few strands fell across her face and I had to move them away...

I loved the way she looked when she wasn't trying to look beautiful.

When she was simply being herself...

IV — There was a softness in being close.

When She Came Closer

And then there were the moments when she came closer...

When my hands found her waist...

When she came into my arms without hesitation...

The way she fit there so naturally...

The way she would give in to my touch,
not pulling away,
just letting herself be held....

I loved looking into her eyes from that close...

Watching them soften...

Watching her face change with every little touch...

I loved the way she trusted me with that closeness...

The way she stayed...

The way she let the moment take over...

V — Every little touch became a memory.

The Kisses

I loved hearing her breathe...

When our breaths fell into the same rhythm...

When the space between us disappeared...

When her lips touched mine...

Slowly.....

Softly.....

Until the kiss became the only thing either of us seemed to remember...

I loved the way her lips felt against mine...

The way we would pause for a moment,
look at each other,
and find our way back to the same kiss.

I loved kissing her forehead...

Her cheek...

And the quiet softness of her neck...

I loved the way she would close her eyes...

The way she would become still beneath a tender touch...

VI — Even the smallest things stayed with him.

The Things That Lingered

I loved the scent of her.......

That familiar scent that belonged only to her.

The kind I could remember even when she wasn't there.

The kind I could recognize her with my eyes closed..

I loved whispering close to her...

The softness of her voice near my ear.

The little things she would say that somehow sounded different when they were meant only for me.

I loved holding her face between my hands....

Looking at her from so close that I could notice every little expression.

The way she would soften beneath my touch....

The way she would stay there for a moment,
as if she didn't want me to let go.

I loved her warmth....

The warmth of her hands in mine...

The warmth beneath my fingertips...

The kind that lingered even after she wasn't there anymore...

I loved the way she would look at me afterward.

That look...

The one that said more than anything she could have whispered.

VII — Love lived in the moments between.

The Softness Between Us

I loved those stolen little moments.

The lingering looks...

The shy smiles...

The almost words...

The way she would come nearer without saying she wanted to.

The way I could feel her relax when she knew she was safe with me.

I loved the softness between us...

The kind that didn't need to be rushed.

The kind that lived in a touch held a second longer,
a forehead resting against mine,
a hand finding mine in the dark....

I loved the way we could be playful one moment...
and completely quiet the next.

I loved how familiar her touch became....

How natural it felt to hold her....

To have her close....

To look at her and think,

How did someone become this beautiful to me???

VIII — Even distance could not change it.

Everything That Was Her

I loved her when she was far away...

I loved her when she was beside me...

But there was something about having her there..

hearing her breathe,
remembering her scent,
watching her eyes meet mine..

that made everything else disappear...

And maybe those are the things I remember most....

IX — ALL THE LITTLE THINGS

All of Her.

Her smile....

Her strange little faces...

Her nonsense...

Her laughter...

Her tears...

Her silence....

Her noise...

Her lips...

Her eyes...

Her whispers...

Her scent...

Her touch...

The way she fit into my arms...

The way she looked at me...

The way she stayed...

All those little things that were simply her,

and somehow became some of the most beautiful things I ever knew...
End of Chapter VIII.
