THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter VI

The One Want

Come closer...
Let the distance disappear...
Begin reading ↓
I — The distance between us.

Come Closer

Come closer....
Not because I need to hold u... but because I want to know
..what becomes of the air, when there is no distance left
for either of us to hide inside....

Come closer...

Let me find your hand
turn it gently within mine...

as though somewhere in them there is a secret...
I was always meant to find....

II — Not hurried. Not demanding.

The First Touch

Let me draw you toward me

by the softness of your waist....
Not hurried...
Not demanding...

Just enough....
To feel the moment
your body understands before your words do...

III — Don't look away.

When Wanting Becomes Visible

And don't look away...
Please don't....
I want your eyes

When I touch you...
I want to see

that brief, unguarded moment

when wanting becomes visible
when your breathing changes,

when your lips fall slightly apart,

when silence begins saying...
what neither of us dares to.

IV — The smallest gesture.

Against Your Cheek

And then let my fingers find your hair.
And catch that single strand
fallen across your face

and move it gently aside.

let my hand remain there.
Against your cheek.
Warm...
Still....

As though touching you

has suddenly become

the only thing in the world

worth being patient for....

V — When silence says everything.

The Distance Between Us

And then
nothing....
Just silence...
The kind that trembles

between two people
who have already said too much
without speaking...

The kind where a kiss
doesn't begin with lips,
but with the distance between us
a distance becoming smaller,
and smaller,
until... even breathing
feels like an invitation....

VI — Somewhere between one breath and the next.

When We Finally Meet

And when we finally meet,
let it be slow.
Let it be unhurried.

Let it be a kiss that doesn't ask
how long it may stay,
only refuses
to leave too soon.

Let the world disappear
somewhere between one breath and the next...

Then your forehead against mine...
Our breathing uneven...
Your fingers still holding me,

as though neither of us
has remembered
how to let go....

VII — Close enough to remember.

The Warmth of You

And let me stay there
close enough to feel
the warmth of you,
close enough to hear the little changes in your breath,
close enough to wonder
how something so quiet
can feel so overwhelming....

And then Let my lips find
the curve where your neck
meets your shoulder

not with haste,
but with the patience
of someone who wants
to remember every second...

And let me breathe you in...
Just you...
The warmth of your skin.
The familiar softness
of your presence.

That quiet scent
that belongs to no one else
and somehow makes desire
feel less like hunger...

VIII — The tenderness beneath the wanting.

The Dangerous Part of Intimacy

Perhaps that's what makes intimacy
so dangerous...

Not the closeness of skin...
Not the heat between bodies...

But the moment
you stop pretending
you don't want to be touched.

The moment you let someone
see the tenderness beneath
the wanting.

The ache beneath
the desire.

IX — THE QUIETEST KIND OF LOVE

To Remain Close.

Because perhaps the most intimate thing two people can do
isn't to lose themselves
in each other

but to remain close...
Breathing together...
Hands lingering...
Eyes refusing to look away...

Neither asking
what happens next...
End of Chapter VI.
