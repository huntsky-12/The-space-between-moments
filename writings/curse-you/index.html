THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter II

An Undying Kind of Hate...

A love that could not survive...
and a hatred that could not forget....
Begin reading ↓
I — The Kind of Love That Refuses to Die

The kind of love that refuses to die.

I have heard of undying love...

The kind that outlives the people who carry it...
The kind that survives funerals, abandonment, and the thousand little deaths that come with being slowly forgotten...
The kind that buries itself so deep beneath your ribs that even time, with all its cruelty, cannot dig it out...
The kind that makes a mockery of your pride, strips your dignity bare, and turns your heart into an accomplice to its own suffering...
The kind that makes you kneel before memories you would otherwise set on fire...
The kind that makes forgetting feel like betrayal and remembering feel like punishment...
The kind that makes you wish you could tear out every memory of her, only to realise that your hands would have to bleed to get to them...
And perhaps I would gladly bleed if it meant I could get you out of me....

I know that kind...

II — I Know That Kind

I know that kind.

I know what it is to want to forget the very eyes I once would have given anything to look into.
To curse the memories I once prayed would last forever.
To find myself remembering the smallest things about someone who couldn't even find enough certainty to stay...

III — I Remember Everything

I remember everything.

I remember the first time I saw you.
I remember the first time you looked into my eyes.
I remember falling for you, and I remember how little it took for you to make me believe that you had fallen too....

I remember your secrets...
Your promises...
Your words...

And I wonder how you could leave pieces of yourself inside someone so carelessly, knowing they'll spend years bleeding through their own skin, trying to get you out...

IV — And I Hate You

And I hate you.

And I hate you...

Not because you left... People leave...
I hate you because you made me believe you wouldn't...
I hate you for loving me just enough to make leaving hurt this much....
I hate you for leaving promises buried beneath my skin, where every attempt to forget you only drives them deeper...

And I hate you for those eyes...
I hate that I still remember them...

And I hate that somewhere inside this wretched heart, there is still a part of me waiting for you...
I hope it dies...
I hope every last bit of it dies...

V — I Don't Want to Keep You There

I don't want to keep you there.

I've heard hate binds people forever...
And Perhaps I am doing you the favour of keeping you alive in my thoughts...
But I don't want to keep you there anymore...

So here's what I wish for you...

VI — What I Wish for You

What I wish for you.

May someone love you with everything they have...
May they see you as the answer to questions they have spent their entire lives asking...
May they make you feel wanted in ways you never thought possible...

And may you be unable to love them...

May you spend nights staring at a ceiling, wondering why the person you would give anything to keep is the same person you cannot bring yourself to hold on to...
May you become desperate for affection and disgusted by your own need for it...
May you want to be understood but find yourself incapable of being honest...
May you want someone close enough to touch and far enough away that you don't have to feel anything...
May you know the humiliation of being loved and having nothing to give in return...

You weren't sure about me...
May uncertainty becomes the only thing that never leaves you...

VII — Everything You Once Made Me Dream About

Everything you once made me dream about.

May you find someone whose hand fits perfectly in yours.
Someone whose eyes make you forget what you were saying.
Someone whose hair you can tuck behind her ears, whose face you can touch, whose presence you can crave...

I hope you get to do everything you once made me dream about...

But when she needs you, I hope you become everything she cannot depend on...
When she cries, may you have no comfort to offer...
When she breaks, may you be the last person she can turn to...
When she asks you to stay, may you feel that familiar urge to disappear...

And when she finally stops loving you, I hope it hurts...
I hope you watch it happen...
I hope you see the disgust replacing the affection, the exhaustion replacing the hope, the indifference replacing the desperate need to be loved by you...

I hope you become painfully aware of every little thing you destroyed while convincing yourself you weren't ready...

I hope you spend your life being loved by people you cannot love, missing people you cannot keep, and regretting things you cannot undo...

VIII — MAY YOU REMEMBER ME

May you remember me.

And I hope you remember me...

I hope you remember that I once wanted to be the person who knew all your little secrets...
That I wanted to be there when you couldn't sleep, when you were afraid...
That I wanted the ordinary, ugly, unremarkable parts of you just as much as the parts you showed me...

I hope you remember how easily I would have stayed...
And I hope that memory makes you sick...

And after everything...

May you rot
in your almosts...

End of Chapter II.
