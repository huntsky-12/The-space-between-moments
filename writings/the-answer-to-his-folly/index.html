THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Part II

The Answer to His Folly

What followed was never quite
what he had dared to ask for...
Begin reading ↓
I — Providence had answered without ceremony.

The Answer, Quietly

And perhaps...
his prayer was heard.

Not with a miracle
falling from the sky....

Not with the universe
announcing that it had finally
decided to be kind to him...

It happened quietly...
She chose him.

And somehow...
that was more beautiful
than anything he had ever prayed for...

She loved him too...
Really loved him...

And the strangest part was...
it felt nothing like
the grand love stories
he had once imagined.

It was smaller...
Softer...
Almost ordinary...
And somehow...
that made it feel
even more impossible.

II — She had taken note of the smallest things.

The Particulars of Being Known

It was in the way
she remembered things
he had forgotten telling her...

The little details
he never thought anyone noticed...

The things he liked...
The things that irritated him...
The things he pretended
didn't bother him...

She knew them.
And she remembered...

Sometimes she would say something...
and he would just look at her
for a second longer...
wondering how someone
could know him this well
and still look at him
with that much warmth.

III — They had little need of words.

A Language of Their Own

There were conversations
that wandered nowhere...

One story becoming another...
A stupid joke...
A disagreement...
A sudden silence...
And somewhere between all of it...

they had created a language
that belonged only to them.

Her face spoke...
His eyes answered...
A raised eyebrow...
A small smile...
A look that lasted
just a little too long...

They could say things
without saying anything...

And sometimes...
words simply weren't enough...

IV — Affection had its own little amusements.

The Little Affections

She would sometimes say,
“I love you...”
for no reason at all...

Just to watch
the way his face changed...

Just to see that little happiness...
she knew she could bring out of him
with three simple words...

And then she would smile...
as though making him happy
was something she secretly enjoyed.

Sometimes...
she would say I hate you...
with that little look in her eyes...
just to make him fall for her...
all over again...

Sometimes she would get jealous...
Just enough to make it obvious
that "You r mine..."

And he would pretend
not to notice...
just to see her get
a little more annoyed...

She would complain...
scold him...
tell him how impossible he was...

And somehow...
he would sit there smiling...
because even her anger
felt like love....

V — She remained when the day was unkind.

The Consolation of Her Company

There were days...
when he had nothing left...

Days when everything
seemed to be going wrong...
And she would still be there...
Not always with answers...

Sometimes she didn't know
what to say either...
Sometimes...
she simply stayed...

And there were moments
when he knew they were going
to lose...

He knew it...
She knew it...

There was nothing
either of them could do...
And still...
she would stand beside him...
Fighting alongside him...
just to keep him company...
just so he wouldn't have
to face it alone...

And somehow...
that meant more to him
than winning ever could...

VI — Happiness had become an ordinary affair.

The Ordinary Course of Things

And there were moments when...he would wonder
how the girl he had spent so long
imagining...
was standing right there...
laughing at something...
complaining about something...
telling him some meaningless story
that he would secretly remember
for the rest of the day.

And he loved that...
The meaninglessness of it.
The fact that love
didn't always need beautiful moments.

Sometimes...
it was just her voice
filling an ordinary afternoon...

Sometimes...
it was the way she would say his name
when she wanted his attention...

And sometimes... he would look at her...
and feel something inside him
soften...

because this...
this was the love
he had been dying for.

A world where he could tell her
about his worst day...
and hear her say,
“Come here...”

A world where she could have
a terrible day...
and still let him be there for her.

A world where they could disagree...
be annoyed...
laugh...
forgive...
and continue...
without either of them
having to pretend.

A love with room to breathe...
room to be imperfect...
room to be human...

VII — He scarcely dared trust his good fortune.

A Fortune He Dared Not Question

And again he would look at her...
almost disbelieving...
almost afraid
that if he looked away
for too long...
the universe might realise
its mistake...
and take it all back.

So he held onto
the little moments...

The ones no one else
would think twice about...

Her hand finding his
without thought...

Her voice becoming softer
when she spoke to him...

The quiet smile she gave him
when she knew exactly
what he was thinking...

the silence afterward
could feel just as full.

He had once thought
a miracle had to be something
the whole world could see.

But Nahh...

Sometimes...
a miracle is private....

And he was living a miracle

VIII — A PRIVATE MIRACLE

And Perhaps...

And sometimes...
he would hold her close...
feel her breathing against him...
and remember that once...
he had stood in a temple...
with folded hands...
and asked...

“Can I have her????”

He had asked for a miracle...

And perhaps...
that was all he had ever wanted.
Her...
Him...
Them together...
Simply...
Happily...
And finally together....
End of Part II.
