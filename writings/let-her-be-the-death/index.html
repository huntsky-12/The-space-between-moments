Chapter V

Let Her Be the Death...

If she can be my death...
then I think I'm ready...
Begin reading ↓

Have you ever seen someone and thought that she is gonna be the death of you?

I saw her today...

I saw her today and felt like
can death be this beautiful?
If yes...
if she can be my death...
Then I think I'm ready to die....

But to die, I have a condition...

God, oh God...
Let her be the hand that puts the knife inside my heart...
and let her be the arms I die in...

Let her be the only eyes that cry for me when I'm gone...

Let her be the only one I see..
the only one I touch...
when I'm breathing my last breath...

And when the pain comes...
let me look at her...
Let me forget the wound..
and remember only her face...

Let the last thing my eyes hold
be the same thing
that made me want to close them forever....

And when the blood leaves me...
let it carry every thought of her with it...
Every glance...
Every smile...
Every moment I imagined but never lived...
Let it take everything,
except her face...
I want that to remain...
Even when everything else begins to disappear...

Let my hands grow cold,
while hers are still warm...

Let my voice become a whisper
while hers is the last voice I hear...

Let my heart become still
while hers is the only heart..
I can feel beating against me....

And if she cries,
let me know that she cried for me.
Just once.
Just enough to know
that my absence left a wound
where my presence once lived....

Let my ears hear her cry...
Let my skin feel her tears...

Let every tear that falls from her eyes
land on the skin
that is slowly forgetting how to feel...

Then let her hold my face...

Let her look into my eyes
as if she could find me somewhere in them...

Let her call my name...
once...
then again...
as if saying it enough times
could keep me here....

Let her voice tremble...
While mine disappear.

Let there be a thousand things
I want to tell her...
and not a single breath
left to say them...

Let my lips remember her name
even when they can no longer speak it...

Let her come closer
when I begin to slip away...

Let me feel her heartbeat
against my chest...
Warm...
Alive....
While mine grows quieter beneath it...

Then let her hold me tighter
as if she could make time stop...

Let her refuse to let go...

Let me wish I could stay...
Let that wish hurt...
Let it hurt so badly
that even death feels merciful...

And when my eyes begin to close,
let me fight them...

Just for her...
Just long enough
to see her one last time...

Because I don't want darkness
to be the last thing I see....
I want her...
Her eyes...
Her face...
That little smile...

Let that be what follows me
into whatever comes after....
And when my fingers finally lose their strength,
let them still be holding hers....
Let them forget how to move,
but not how she felt....

And then...
let her call my name again...
Let me hear it...
faintly,
Let me try to answer....
Let my lips move,
but let nothing come out....
Let her realize,
I'm slipping away.
And let me hate
that I cannot answer her....
Let that be the last pain
I ever feel....

And then...

And then let me die....
End Of Chapter V
