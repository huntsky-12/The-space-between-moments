INSANITY IS A BANE.
Chapter IV

The Closure

I think I finally understand.
It's not going to happen….
Begin reading ↓
I — I Finally Understand

It's not going to happen….

I think I finally understand.
It's not going to happen….

Not someday.
Not when the timing is right.
Not when I become a little better,a little braver,
a little more like the person
I think she could love.

Just, not me…..

And I think
I've known this for a while.
I just kept finding reasons, not to believe it.

A smile…..
A conversation….
A moment that lasted
a second longer than usual….
I'd take those little things, and turn them into hope.

And hope...
hope is a dangerous thing
when you're the only one holding it.
So I'm letting it go….
Not because, I stopped caring.

God, nooooo…..

I wish it was that easy….
I'm letting it go
because I finally understand....

II — Letting Go

Wanting something badly doesn't make it yours.

I'm letting it go
because I finally understand....
that wanting something badly
doesn't make it yours.
And she doesn't owe me, the ending I imagined.
She doesn't owe me, an explanation.
She doesn't owe me, a chance.
She doesn't even owe me, the feeling
I spent so long hoping for....

And somehow...

accepting that
hurts more than rejection ever could.
Because there was never really
anything to reject.
There was just me…
waiting………

Waiting for a story, that was never being written.

So this is my closure…
Not a goodbye to her.
She never really belonged to me
in the first place.
It's a goodbye, to the version of me, who kept waiting….

III — The One Who Kept Waiting

There is no tomorrow where that suddenly happens.

It's a goodbye, The one who looked for signs…
The one who replayed conversations.
The one who thought, maybe tomorrow
she would see him differently.
There is no tomorrow, where that suddenly happens.

And I have to stop, living inside it....

I wish I could say
I'm okay now.
I'm not…..

Some days I'll still look for her.
Some days I'll still wonder
what we could have been.
Some days, I'll almost forget
and then something small, will bring everything back.

And I'll hurt…
I'll probably hurt
for longer than I want to….

But I think
that's okay.
Maybe moving on, doesn't mean forgetting.
Maybe it doesn't mean
I stop loving her....

Maybe it just means, I stop asking love
to give me something, it was never meant to give....

.

IV — Putting the Ending Here

Someone has to….

So...
this is me, putting the ending here.

Not because I wanted it.
But because
someone has to….
And if she was never going to
close this chapter with me,
then I will....

I'll close it myself….
I'll fold away
all the things I never said.
All the possibilities….
All the little what ifs..

And I'll carry the memory
without carrying the hope.

Because I loved her….
Maybe I still do….
But I can't keep standing
at a door
that was never going to open....

So this is goodbye….

V — THE ENDING I DIDN'T WANT

My closure…

So this is goodbye….
Not to her….
To waiting for her….

And maybe...
that's the closest thing
to closure
I'll ever get……………..
End of Chapter IV
