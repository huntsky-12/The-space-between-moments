THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter III

The Ruin of Loving Her

They asked him to write
about her beauty...
Begin reading ↓
I — He loved watching them.

The Eyes

They asked him... to write about something beautiful...

He looked at her for a moment... as though the answer had been sitting in front of him all along...

And perhaps it had...

It was in the way her eyes moved before she ever said a word. ..The way they would find his... stay there just long enough to make him forget himself... and then slip away as though they had done nothing at all... His eyes followed hers without permission... Hers played aankh-micholi with his... and his had already surrendered to the game...

He loved watching them...

The way they opened when something caught her attention... the way they narrowed when she teased him... the way they closed when she laughed... He wanted to learn that rhythm... not because he needed to understand it, but because he wanted to watch it forever... If those eyes could open and close a thousand times in a day... he wanted to be there for every one of them...

II — As though those eyes needed anything more.

The Kajal and the Glasses

And then she would put on her kajal...

As though those eyes .... Those eyes of her....needed anything more...

They didn't...

They were already beautiful enough... to make the night feel incomplete without them... But the kajal had a strange talent... it didn't create anything... it simply complimented what was already there... It made those eyes a little darker... a little sharper... a little prettier... as if someone had taken something already perfect and decided to underline it...

Unnecessary...

Yet somehow, completely right...

And then came the glasses... her favourite little attempt at hiding all of it...

She would put them on... as though a simple pair of frames could make those eyes disappear...

They couldn't...

He would still find them behind the glass... still catch them moving... still watch them return to his... and sometimes wonder how she could possibly believe she was hiding anything...

III — They always gave her away.

The Smile and the Cheeks

Then she would smile...

And somehow... the eyes were no longer the thing he was looking at...

Her smile had that little bit of shyness in it... the kind that appeared only when she realized she had been noticed... It would begin at her lips and slowly find its way to her cheeks... painting them a soft rosy red... She could look away... lower her face... pretend to be embarrassed by something else... but her cheeks had already given her away...

They always did...

They became rosy with embarrassment... red with anger...soft with happiness...warm with affection... It was almost unfair how much they could say without her saying anything at all....

And perhaps that was why he loved looking at her face...not because every feature was beautiful by itself... but because they seemed to belong to one another...

Her eyes would make him notice her smile...

Her smile would make her cheeks blush...

Her blush would make her look away...

And when she looked away... he would notice the little things hanging beside her face...

IV — Living the dream he wanted to be.

The Jhumkas

Her jhumkas...

God, her jhumkas...

Did she really need them???

Those little silver pink pieces ...swung beside her neck with every movement she made... as though they had somehow been given the life he wished he had... They moved with the pace of her body... swayed when she walked.. trembled when she turned... danced when she laughed...

They got to stay close to her neck...

Close enough to feel the warmth of her skin...

Close enough to disappear into her scent...

Close enough to be touched by her fingers... whenever she reached up to fix them...

Sometimes he looked at those jhumkas... and thought they were living the dream he wanted to be...

They didn't need a reason to be close to her...

They simply were...

And perhaps... that was the only thing he envied about them...

V — Even the ordinary became beautiful.

The Little Things

Because then she would turn her face again... and his eyes would leave the jhumkas and fall upon her lips...

Those rosy little things...

Soft like rose petals... warm like something meant to be held in winter... He loved the way they moved when she spoke... the way they curved when she smiled... the way they called his name... and somehow made it sound like it belonged to her...

Her lips said his name...

His heart answered...

Even her nose had become something he found beautiful... not because it needed to be perfect...but because it was hers... Sitting quietly between those eyes and those lips...rising and falling with every breath she took.. somehow making even something as ordinary as breathing feel like another little thing worth watching...

And then his eyes would return to her cheeks...

Those same cheeks that had turned pink when she was shy...

Those same cheeks that burned red when she was angry...

Those same cheeks that became soft and warm when she was happy...

He loved that they changed with her...

That they betrayed her...

That they made visible all the little things she sometimes tried to hide...

VI — The one who could destroy him.

The Ruin of Loving Her

And somewhere between watching her eyes move and watching her smile... between noticing the kajal and envying the jhumkas... between listening to her lips call his name and watching the colour rise into her cheeks... he realized

That she is the one...

The one who could destroy him without ever raising a hand...

The one who could turn all the pieces of him into ruins... simply by looking at him the way she did...

That she could make a home out of his heart... and then with the same smile burn the whole thing down....

That she could leave him with nothing but her memories... and somehow... he knew he would still call that nothing everything...

VII — THE UNAVOIDABLE

And Yet.

Tabah kar degi woh...

He knew it...

And yet couldn't stop himself from loving her.....
End of Chapter III
