THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter IV

On Either Side

Two hearts...
carrying the same secret...

I — They loved each other.

The Same Secret

They loved each other...

And that should have made everything easy....

Instead... it made everything frightening...

Because both of them knew what they felt...
Neither knew what the other would do with the truth...

So... they carried the same secret in two different hearts...
each waiting for the other to become careless enough to reveal it...

He didn't want to be the fool who mistook kindness for love...
And she didn't want to be the girl who turned a few beautiful moments into a confession...

They weren't afraid of loving...
They were afraid of being wrong about being loved...

So they became careful...
Careful with their words...
Careful with their eyes...
Careful with every little thing that might say more than they were ready to admit...

II — Somehow, they always found each other.

Finding Each Other

And yet... somehow, they always found each other...

A room could be overflowing with people....
His eyes would wander through the crowd without knowing what they were looking for...
Then they would find her...
And somewhere across the room... she would have already found him...

For that brief second... it felt as though the rest of the room had stepped out of the way...

Their eyes met...
Her heart would race...
His would stop...

Then one of them would look away...
Too quickly...
Almost as though being caught looking at the person you love
was something shameful...

But neither really looked away...
Not inside...
They noticed each other more than anyone else...

He remembered the things she said without realizing he had remembered them...
She noticed the smallest changes in him...

They could tell when something was wrong...
Could tell when the other was pretending...
They knew each other's moods from across a room...
They knew the little things...
And still... neither said the one thing they both wanted to hear...

I feel it too...

III — Close enough to want.

The Distance Between Them

Sometimes ...there was a seat beside one of them...
They both wanted it...
Neither took it...

Because wanting to sit beside someone is easy..
until you start wondering whether they want you there too...

And even if they did sit together...
what then???

How were they supposed to act... as though nothing was happening
with the person whose nearness already made everything inside them restless??

A shoulder nearby...
A hand within reach...
The unbearable awareness of being this close...

They had imagined it countless times...
They had never imagined how frightening it would be when it finally became possible...

So they stayed apart...
Not because they wanted the distance...
Because they didn't trust themselves with the closeness...

IV — The Life They Had Imagined

An Entire Lifetime.

They wanted to hold hands...
They wanted to walk together...
They wanted to share a home someday...

In their minds... they had already made one shared mornings, little arguments, her things slowly finding their way into his spaces...

A whole life waiting for them...
And yet, neither could manage the first step toward it...

Because neither wanted to be the first to say it...
The first one would have to risk being wrong...
The first one would have to stand there after saying...

I love you..............

and wait...
Wait to see whether the other person smiled...
or went quiet...
or gently broke something that had taken so long to build...

They were terrified of looking foolish..

Of discovering that all those smiles, conversations, little gestures and remembered details had been ordinary to the other person....

But what greater fools were they...
two people already in love...
still trying to preserve their dignity...
as though love has ever had dignity???

They kept choosing safety...

And love kept asking for a little stupidity...
A little courage...
A little embarrassment...
Anything that would let it breathe...

V — Somewhere, the universe knew.

Wishes Left to the Sky

So they left some of their wishes to the sky...

A star fell across his sky once.
He watched it disappear...
and wished for her
before the darkness could take it...

And when 11:11 found her..
she would close her eyes
and leave one wish with the universe for
him...

VI — Two banks of the same river.

On Either Side

And Perhaps... that was all they would ever be...

Two people carrying the same love..
and never finding the courage to place it between them...

Perhaps... they would remain strangers
with an entire lifetime hiding behind their eyes...

Like two banks of the same river...

running beside each other...
close enough to hear the other's water...
never close enough to touch...

And perhaps... when the river finally reached its end...
when all its wandering was over,
the banks would meet...

But by then...
there would be nowhere left to go..
nothing left to become...
and no reason for the meeting anymore...

VII — THE QUIET TRAGEDY

And only then...

Just the quiet tragedy...

of two people who had spent their whole lives...

beside each other...

and only found each other...
when it no longer mattered...
The End.
