THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter VIII

The Folly of Asking

He had never been a man of faith...
though love, it seemed, had other arrangements...
Begin reading ↓
I — He never believed someone was listening.

Of Heavens and Other Silences

He never believed in skies
that listened...

He had never understood
how people could stand beneath the endless blue...
fold their hands...
close their eyes...
and believe that... Somewhere beyond all of it...
there was someone listening
to the things... they couldn't say to anyone else....

To him,
the sky was only the sky....

Beautiful, sometimes...
Terrifying, sometimes....
But empty....

A temple was only a temple...
Stone carved into gods..
bells hanging from ceilings...
incense disappearing into air...

He could never understand
how a folded hand
could change a person's fate...
How a whispered wish
could become a miracle....

How people could ask the universe
for something...
And actually believe
the universe might answer...

He had never asked...
Not once....

Life had taken things from him before...
He had lost...
He had failed...
He had stood in situations..
where he would have given anything
for things to turn out differently...

Still... he never prayed...

He would rather accept the cruelest answer
than ask a question
to a God he wasn't sure existed...

He was stubborn like that...

II — Love found the one place his stubbornness could not survive.

When the Heart Took Leave of Reason

Then he loved her...

And somehow...
love found the one place
where his stubbornness could not survive...

Because loving her
was different from wanting something...

Wanting was simple...
You could work for it...
Fight for it...
Reach for it...

But how do you work
for someone's heart???

How do you fight
for someone's choice...
without turning love
into something selfish???

He could give her everything he had...
and still not be enough...

He could spend years
loving her carefully..
and she could still wake up one morning
and choose another man...

There was nothing he could do about it...

And that helplessness
slowly began eating him alive...

III — He wanted her heart to remain free.

Of Her Own Free Will

He loved her enough
to know that her heart
had to remain free...

If she chose him,
he wanted it to be because
she wanted him...

Not because he had convinced her...
Or because fate had pushed her
into his arms...

He wanted her choice.

Her real...
impossible...
beautiful choice...

And he had no way
of asking for it...

IV — For the first time, he looked up.

An Unbidden Question

So one day...
he found himself looking at the sky
longer than usual...

And for the first time...
the thought came to him...

What if there really is someone up there???

He almost laughed at himself...

After all these years?
Now???
Now he wanted God???

And Perhaps.
Perhaps God has found a way to humble him...

V — Just this once. Just for her.

An Impertinent Petition

But he wanted to believe...
Just this once...
Just for her...

So he let himself believe it...
Just for a moment...

So...

He walked into a temple.

And stood there quietly,
not knowing the right words,
not knowing what people usually ask for...

He didn't know how to pray...

And he just stood there
like a fool..
with his hands folded...

and asked

“Can I have her????”

And thought that perhaps the god would understand...
what he really meant...

Not give her to him...
Not make her love him...

Just...

“Can You please let the story
I've been living in my head
become real??"

VI — The life he had been living in his head.

A Life Yet Unbestowed

Could she be the one
who comes home to him...

Could she be the one
who falls asleep beside him..

Could she be the one
who looks into his eyes...
and somehow knows
everything he has never been brave enough
to say...

Could there really be a morning
where he wakes up
and she is there...

A life where she falls into his arms
as though she has always known
that is where she belongs....

VII — He knew none of it was guaranteed.

Against the Odds of Providence

And He knew none of this
was guaranteed...

He knew love didn't work like that...

He knew a prayer...
was only a prayer...

And yet...
for the first time in his life...
he wanted to believe otherwise...

He wanted to believe
that somewhere...
somehow...
a folded pair of hands
could change a future...

That a whispered wish
could travel farther
than he ever could...

That perhaps miracles
weren't impossible...

That perhaps the universe
sometimes listened...

That perhaps...
just once...
the story he had written
inside his heart
could become the life
he woke up to...

And perhaps that was
the most helpless thing about him now...

VIII — THE FOLLY OF BELIEVING

He Wanted to Believe.

He didn't know if God was real...
He didn't know if prayers worked...
He didn't know if the sky
could hear a single word
spoken beneath it...

But he wanted to believe...

Not because someone had convinced him....

Not because he had found faith...

But because he loved her
enough... to believe
in something he had spent
his whole life calling impossible....
End of Chapter VIII.
