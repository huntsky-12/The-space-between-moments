THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter I

The Dream of You

The life I imagine
when I imagine you....
Begin reading ↓
I — A Morning That Feels Like Forever

A morning that feels like forever.

I think my favourite dream of you
isn't really a dream at all...

It's just a morning...

Waking up with you in my arms,
before the world has had the chance to ask anything of us....

For a few quiet seconds...
there is only your warmth against me..
your hair resting somewhere between my fingers and your face,
and the softness of your breathing....

And then you open your eyes...

Sleepy...
A little lost...
Still somewhere between dreaming and waking....

And I look at you....

I think I'd forget how mornings are supposed to begin....

Because how could I possibly want coffee,
sunlight,
or anything else...
when the first thing I get to see
is you looking at me with those half-asleep eyes???

And then that smile....

God...

I think I'd want to live my best mornings
inside that smile...

I'd touch your face just because you're there...
Trace my fingers along your cheek...
Move your hair away from your eyes...

And then, because I know I wouldn't be able to resist,
I'd tickle you until that sleepy little smile
turns into laughter,
until you bury your face somewhere against me,
pretending you're annoyed....

And somehow we'd end up standing beside each other...
brushing our teeth...
still half asleep..
looking at each other through the mirror..
like two people who haven't quite understood
how they got this lucky...

II — Loving You in the Little Things

Loving you in the little things.

Then you'd start getting ready...

And I'd sit there on the bed...

Just watching...

Watching you fix your hair...
Watching you choose your clothes...
Watching you stand in front of the mirror
and somehow make the whole room feel beautiful
without even trying....

And I'd wonder...

Why are you even doing all this?

You're already the most beautiful woman...
I've ever seen....

You don't need the dress...
You don't need the mirror...
You don't need anything...

You could stand there
with sleepy eyes and messy hair,
and I'd still be sitting on that bed
trying to understand
how someone can look like home....

I'd get up eventually...

Dry your hair...

Gather it gently...
Put the little rubber around it...
Fix the strands that keep escaping...

Straighten your dress...

Then I'd kneel in front of you
and take your socks in my hands,
slipping them gently over your feet
as though even the smallest part of caring for you
were something precious to me....

Then I'd lift your shoe onto your foot,
hold it carefully..tie the laces slowly...

And while my fingers moved,
I'd look up at you....

You'd be looking down at me
with that soft smile,
your eyes full of love,
and for a moment,
the whole world would disappear...

There would be nothing ordinary about it then...

Not you standing there...
Not me kneeling at your feet...
Not the way your smile would make my heart feel too full...

Because I wouldn't just be tying your laces...

I'd be loving you
in one of the simplest,
most beautiful ways I know...

And I'd think
that if I could spend my life
looking up at you like that,
with your love shining back at me,
I would never need anything more...

Because somehow,
these are the things I'd remember...

Just your feet in my hands
on an ordinary morning..

Your hair between my fingers..

Your dress that needed fixing...

Your hand finding mine
when we finally walked out..

III — When the World Becomes Just You

When the world becomes just you.

And whenever your fingers were cold,
I'd hold them between my palms
and rub them slowly
until the warmth returned...

I'd walk beside you...

Sometimes behind you...

Following you without even thinking about it...

Because when you're there,
my eyes don't really know
where else to go...

The world could be moving around us,
people could be talking,
cars could be passing...

and somehow,
I'd still only see you...

Just you...

IV — Being There When the Day Gets Heavy

Being there when the day gets heavy.

And then the day would happen...

A long one...

A tiring one...

And you'd come back to me
with that exhaustion sitting quietly
on your face...

And I'd know...

I'd make the food you like...
Put on the songs you love...
Let the room become quiet around us...

I'd sit beside you
and press the tiredness out of your legs
slowly,
patiently,
as though I could somehow
undo everything the day had taken from you...

Then oil in your hair...

My fingers moving slowly through it...

The kind of silence
that doesn't need anything said...

Just you,
resting there,
finally letting yourself be tired...

And eventually,
I'd pull you into my arms...

Your head against my chest...

Your body becoming heavier
as sleep slowly finds you...

And I'd stay there...

Not moving...

Just feeling your warmth,
breathing in your scent,
watching those tired eyes
fight sleep for a few more seconds...

V — The Space Between Sleep and Love

The space between sleep and love.

And then you'd look at me...

Red cheeks...
Sleepy eyes...
Rosy lips...

And maybe,
in that little space between being awake
and disappearing into sleep...

you'd ask me to love you....

And I'd look at you
and think...

what else have I been doing
all this time???

I'd gently move closer to you..
slowly enough to let the moment breathe...
and press my lips against yours.

At first, softly...

Almost like a question...

And then you'd meet me there..
your hand finding my shirt..
your fingers holding on as though you were afraid,
the moment might disappear...

Our breathing would become uneven,
quiet and broken between kisses..
and the whole room would blur around us.

There would be no day left...
No tiredness...
No thoughts waiting to be finished...

Only your lips against mine...
your warmth beneath my hands..
and the way we would lose ourselves
in something neither of us needed to explain...

I'd kiss you slowly,
then pull back just enough
to look at you...

Your eyes would be half closed.
Your cheeks warm...
Your lips still parted...

And I'd smile...
because even then,
you'd look like the most beautiful thing
I've ever been allowed to hold...

Then I'd kiss you again...

More tenderly this time...

As though I could pour every word I had never said
into that one moment...

And you'd rest your forehead against mine,
both of us trying to catch our breath,
laughing softly because somehow
even silence felt too full...

I'd hold your face in my hands
and whisper that I love you...

Not because you asked...

Not because the moment demanded it...

But because with you so close,
the words would have nowhere else to go....

And you'd look at me
with that sleepy, softened smile,
as though you already knew...

VI — An Ordinary Life Worth Dreaming Of

An ordinary life worth dreaming of.

Then I'd kiss your forehead
and hold you closer...

Until your breathing becomes slow...

Until the room becomes completely still...

Until the woman I spent the entire day
following with my eyes
is finally asleep in my arms...

And maybe that's the whole dream...

No castles...
No perfect sunsets...
No extraordinary life...

Just you.... And you...

In my arms at the beginning of the day...
Your hand in mine through it...
Your tired head against me at the end...

And somewhere between..
your sleepy eyes in the morning
and your sleepy eyes at night,..

I'd get to live
an entire lifetime...

VII — IF THIS IS ALL LIFE GIVES ME

If this is all life gives me.

I think... that's all I've ever wanted...

Not a perfect life...

Just a life..
where every ordinary moment
somehow has you in it...
End of Chapter I
