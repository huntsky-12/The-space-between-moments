INSANITY IS A BANE.
Chapter I

Dilkashi

Sometimes, it isn't about someone becoming beautiful.
It's about your heart finally learning to see the beauty that was already there.
Begin reading ↓
I — The First Glance

The beginning isn't always obvious.

Dilkashi.... not something that always begins when you first meet someone.

Sometimes, it does....

You see her for the first time, and you don't know what it is that makes you look twice.

Maybe it's her smile....

Maybe it's the softness in her voice, the way she carries herself, or the way a few loose strands of hair fall across her face when she isn't paying attention....

You don't know anything about her yet.

And still, something in you pauses....

You look again....

Perhaps that is Dilkashi....

When someone you barely know somehow manages to catch a little piece of your heart....

II — Familiarity

Sometimes, it takes longer.

But sometimes, it takes longer....

You meet someone, and nothing happens.

You talk.

You laugh.

You pass each other by.

Days become weeks, weeks become months, and they remain just another familiar face in your life.

Until one ordinary day, something changes....

Maybe she laughs at something you said....

Maybe you hear her voice from across the room and find yourself listening for it.

Maybe you notice the way she looks at people when they speak to her, giving them her whole attention.

Or perhaps nothing happens at all....

Perhaps you simply look at her one day and realize....

When did I start noticing her like this???

She hasn't changed.

Maybe you have....

III — Learning to See

The beauty was always there.

And that is the strange beauty of Dilkashi....

Sometimes, it isn't about someone becoming beautiful.

It's about your heart finally learning to see the beauty that was already there.

A face you've seen a hundred times suddenly feels unfamiliar in the most beautiful way....

A voice you've heard before somehow stays with you.

A presence you once took for granted becomes the presence your eyes quietly search for....

You begin remembering little things about her without meaning to.

The way she smiles when she's trying not to laugh.

The things she says and forgets a moment later.

The little habits she probably doesn't know anyone notices.

And somewhere between noticing and remembering, Dilkashi begins to grow....

IV — Something About Her

You don't decide to be drawn to her.

You simply are....

Sometimes it's her beauty.

Sometimes her kindness.

Sometimes her confidence, her innocence, her madness.

Sometimes it is something so small that even you can't explain it.

There is just something about her that makes your heart lean a little closer....

V — Before Love

Before you call it love.

And perhaps that is what makes Dilkashi so innocent....

You don't want anything from her yet.

You don't know if you could ever have her.

You don't even know if what you feel will become love.

You just want to know her.

Not her name.

Not the things everyone else knows about her.

Her.

The person she is when nobody is watching.

What makes her laugh when she's alone.

What she thinks about on quiet nights.

What kind of dreams she keeps to herself.

And slowly, without realizing it, curiosity begins to feel a lot like affection.

The thought you shouldn't have

What would it be
like to know her?

What would it be like if she knew me too?

VI — Possibility

You find yourself wondering....

What would it be like to know her?

What would it be like if she knew me too?

And then, one day, a thought comes quietly enough that you almost don't notice it.

What if there could be an us???

You smile at the thought.

Then you push it away....

Then you find yourself thinking about it again.

VII — No Promises

Dilkashi doesn't promise anything.

Because Dilkashi doesn't arrive with promises....

It doesn't tell you that she will stay.

It doesn't tell you that she will love you back.

It doesn't even tell you that this feeling has a future.

It simply makes one person different from everyone else.

And maybe that's enough.
VIII — The Quiet Beginning

Before love asks you to stay.

Because before love asks you to stay,

before longing teaches you what absence feels like,

before you begin imagining a future that may never happen....

there is only this quiet beginning....

A glance that lingers.

A voice you remember.

A smile you find yourself waiting for.

A presence that makes an ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

A heart that has begun, very quietly,

to move in someone's direction.

Perhaps That Is Dilkashi

Perhaps that is Dilkashi....

Not knowing where the road leads.

Not knowing whether there will ever be an us....

Only knowing that, somehow,

among all the people you've met,

there is one person

you suddenly want to see again.

And maybe that's how some of the most beautiful stories begin....

not with I love you....,

but with a heart whispering,

I don't know what this is yet...
but I think I'd like to know you a little more....
End of Chapter I
