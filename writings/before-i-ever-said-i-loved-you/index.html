THE SPACE BETWEEN MOMENTS.
Chapter VII

Before I Ever Said I Loved You

I've seen a thousand beautiful things...
but my eyes keep finding yours...
Begin reading ↓
I — They make me forget.

What Your Eyes Do To Me

I don't think you'll ever understand
what your eyes do to me....

Not because they're beautiful...

That's too simple...

Everyone can see that..

With your glasses....
without your glasses....
I don't think you've ever given my eyes
a version of you they didn't like....

But I'm talking about the way... they make me forget...
what I was about to say.
The way I sometimes look at you...
and suddenly become conscious of myself...
of where I'm standing,
of what my hands are doing,
of how close I am to you....

Sometimes your eyes make me want to look away....

Not because I don't want you to see me,
but because when you look at me....
I feel as though you can see every part of me
I've spent years trying to hide....

And strangely,
there's no one I'd rather be seen by than you....

II — You see what I hide.

Being Seen

You have this way of looking at me...
that feels almost like you've reached inside me
without ever touching me....

It intimidates me....

And somehow,
I love that it does....

Because I don't think anyone else
could make me feel so small
and so safe
at the same time....

III — I've started noticing everything.

Learning Your Eyes

I've started learning your eyes...

I know... when you've had a bad day
even when you tell me you're fine...

I know... when something is bothering you...
because there is a certain quietness in them...

I know when you're excited....

I know because your pupils give you away....

They open a little wider,
as if even your eyes
are trying to get closer
to whatever has made you happy....

And when you're stressed,
I see them change....

When you're scared...
you close them so tightly.

And every time you do,
some stupid part of me thinks...

please don't.

Don't hide them from me...

Let me see your fear...

Let me see the parts of you
that you think aren't beautiful.

I want those too....
Cause whatever comes from u is pretty to me...

IV — Your eyes have never been good at lying.

Everything They Confess

And Maybe that's what I've fallen in love with....

Not just your eyes...

Everything they confess
when you're trying not to confess anything...

Because your eyes
have never been very good at lying to me....

Your lips can say,

I'm okay...

Your eyes never do....

And I don't know if you realize
how much that means to me...

Because sometimes
you don't have to tell me what happened...

You just look at me...

And somehow I know...

I know you've been tired...

I know you've been carrying something...

I know you need someone to stay...

So I stay....

V — I see myself differently through you.

My Favourite Mirror

I think your eyes became my favourite mirror
without me even realizing it....

Because whenever I look into them,
I don't just see myself...

I see what I am around you...

The softness I don't show anyone...

The nervousness I try to hide...

The way I look at you
like I'm afraid you'll disappear
if I blink for too long...

And maybe that's why
I love looking at you...

Because for a few seconds
I don't have to pretend to be anything...

I can just be the person
who is hopelessly, helplessly
looking at the person he loves...

VI — And God... the world disappears.

When Our Eyes Meet

And then our eyes meet.

And God...

I hate how the world disappears...

People are still talking...

Cars are still moving...

The clock is still moving its hands
around that stupid circle
as if time actually matters....

But it doesn't...
Not then....atleast

Because suddenly there is only you...

And me....

And that tiny distance between our eyes
that somehow feels more intimate
than being close enough to touch...

Sometimes I wonder...
if you know what happens to me in those moments...

How my mind goes completely quiet...

How I forget every clever thing
I thought I would say to you...

How I just stand there
looking at you
thinking...

how can one person possibly be this beautiful?

VII — The one thing I cannot bear to see.

When Your Eyes Fill With Tears

And when your eyes fill with tears...

I don't know why,
but those are the moments
when your beauty scares me the most.

Because I've never been afraid of your anger...

I've never been afraid of your silence.

But tears in your eyes...

those terrify me...

I would do almost anything
to make them disappear....

I'd take every reason for them
if I could...

I'd hold your face in my hands
and wipe them away so carefully
that you wouldn't even feel my fingers.

And sometimes I just want to touch them..

Just once...

Gently...

Take the fallen eyelids from ur eyes ...

And then... ask you to close your eyes
and make a wish...

And maybe I'd spend the rest of my life
trying to become it....

Because that's the ridiculous thing
about loving you...

I don't just want your happiness...

I want to be somewhere inside
the reason you have it...

VIII — I want to be the reason they light up.

The Person You Look For

I want to be the person
whose name makes your eyes light up...

I want to be the person
you look for in a crowded room...

I want to know that somewhere
in this enormous world,
there is a pair of eyes
that still searches for mine...

And when you're happy..

really happy..

I swear your eyes change...

They become brighter...

There is this little light in them
that I can't explain...

And every time I see it,
I fall for you again...

IX — Every version of you lives there.

Every Version of You

Maybe that's why I keep looking...

Maybe I'm greedy...

Maybe I keep looking at your eyes
because every version of you lives there...

The girl who laughs...

The girl who worries...

The girl who gets excited over little things...

The girl who is scared...

The girl who is tired...

The girl who tries to say she's okay
when she isn't...

The girl who looks at me
and makes me forget
there is anyone else in the room...

X — I don't want to stop looking.

Where I Go To Find You

And I don't want to stop...

I don't want to know
what the world looks like
when I'm not looking at you...

Because your eyes aren't just beautiful to me anymore...

They're where I go
when I want to feel close to you...

They're where I find the things
you don't say...

They're where I see the things
you try to hide...

They're where I recognize you...

And maybe that's the strangest part
of loving someone this much...

XI — If only you could see yourself.

Through My Eyes

you've probably never understood
why I look at you the way I do...

Because you've only ever seen yourself
through your own eyes...

You've seen the face in the mirror...

You've noticed the things you don't like...

You've probably wondered
what I could possibly be looking at
for so long....

But I wish, just once,
you could see yourself through mine...

Just for a moment...

Just long enough to understand
why I forget my words...

Why I lose track of time...

Why your tears frighten me...

Why your happiness makes me fall for you again...

Why, even after all this time,
my eyes still find yours
before they find anything else...

XII — WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

Long Before I Admitted It.

And maybe...

just maybe..

they're where I fell in love with you
long before I ever had the courage
to admit that I had....
End of Chapter VII.
